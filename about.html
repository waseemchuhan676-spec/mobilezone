About MobileZone

MobileZone is an independent information portal about mobile phone prices in Pakistan. It lists current prices, confirmed specifications, 5G and budget filters, a PTA tax calculator, a phone finder and a comparison tool — so buyers can shortlist a phone before visiting a shop.

What MobileZone covers

The catalogue covers 63 phone models, of which 56 carry a listed price (prices updated: 29 October 2026). Each phone page shows only confirmed data: price, RAM, storage, main camera, battery and 5G support, plus a photo where one is available. Anything not yet confirmed is labelled as such on the page — it is never filled in with a guess.

Budget pages (under Rs 40,000, 50,000, 70,000 and 100,000), 5G phones and the latest-phones list are generated from the same catalogue, so the numbers on those pages always match the phone pages.

Where prices come from

Listed prices come from official brand sources and reputable Pakistan price trackers, and are marked with their last update date. Older or discontinued models whose current shop price cannot be confirmed are shown as “Price not confirmed” rather than with an estimated figure. See the Editorial Policy for the full sourcing rules.

What MobileZone is not

MobileZone does not sell phones, does not take orders or payments, and is not affiliated with any phone brand, shop, network, or with the PTA. PTA tax figures on this site are estimates from the published FBR duty structure — the official DIRBS system is the final authority. Prices are approximate and change with the market: always confirm the final price at a shop before buying.

Tools on this site

Use the free tools on the homepage: the PTA Tax Calculator, the Smart Finder and Compare Mobiles (up to 3 phones side by side).

Frequently asked questions

Does MobileZone sell mobile phones?
No. MobileZone is an information site only. It does not sell phones, accept orders or take payments.
Are the prices official brand prices?
Listed prices are market prices gathered from official and reputable Pakistan sources, marked with their update date. They are approximate — confirm the final price at a shop.
Is MobileZone affiliated with the PTA?
No. The PTA tax calculator is an independent estimate tool. Final duties are confirmed through the official PTA/DIRBS process.
