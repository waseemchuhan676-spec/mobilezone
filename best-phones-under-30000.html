Our verdicts

Sparx Neo 8 Pro (Rs. 23,499): Cheapest 128GB phone in this list at Rs. 23,499 — the lowest price for 4GB RAM with 128GB storage. The 13MP camera is basic, so pick it for storage and price, not photos.

Tecno Camon 17 (Rs. 24,999): The value pick of this list: 6GB RAM and 128GB storage with a 48MP camera at Rs. 24,999 — the most RAM and storage per rupee under Rs. 30,000.

Tecno Spark 10C (Rs. 24,999): 4GB RAM and 128GB storage at Rs. 24,999, with a 16MP camera. A balanced pick if you want more storage than the Smart 8 without paying more.

Infinix Smart 8 (Rs. 24,999): The cheapest 50MP camera in this list at Rs. 24,999. Storage is 64GB, so it suits buyers who care about photos more than storage.

Infinix Smart 9 (Rs. 26,999): 4GB RAM and 128GB storage at Rs. 26,999. It costs Rs. 3,500 more than the Neo 8 Pro for the same RAM and storage, with a 13MP camera — the Neo 8 Pro is the better-value buy on specs alone.

Samsung Galaxy A06 (Rs. 26,999): The Samsung pick: 4GB RAM, 64GB storage and a 50MP camera at Rs. 26,999. You pay a little more than the Tecno and Infinix options for the Samsung brand — the specs are mid-pack, not the best value here.

Xiaomi Redmi 13C (Rs. 27,499): 4GB RAM, 128GB storage and a 50MP camera at Rs. 27,499 — the cheapest way to get 128GB storage with a 50MP camera in this list.

Tecno Spark 20C (Rs. 27,999): The same 4GB/128GB/50MP combination as the Redmi 13C at Rs. 27,999 — Rs. 500 more. Choose between the two on brand preference, not specs.

Tecno Spark 30C (Rs. 29,999): The strongest specs in this list: 6GB RAM, 128GB storage and a 50MP camera at Rs. 29,999 — the top of the budget, and the only phone here with 6GB RAM and a 50MP camera together.

Xiaomi Redmi 14C (Rs. 29,999): 4GB RAM, 128GB storage and a 50MP camera at Rs. 29,999 — the same combination as the Redmi 13C for Rs. 2,500 more, so the 13C is the better-value buy unless you want the newer model.

Also under Rs. 30,000

Six more phones on MobileZone cost under Rs. 30,000: Xiaomi Redmi A3x (Rs. 18,999), Tecno Spark Go 2 (Rs. 19,999), Xiaomi Redmi A3 (Rs. 20,999), Tecno Spark Go 1 (Rs. 22,999), Tecno Spark Go 3 (Rs. 25,899), Oppo A18 (Rs. 27,999). They did not make the top 10 because they pair 64GB storage with a weaker camera (8MP or 13MP) — every phone in the top 10 offers 128GB storage or a 50MP camera, or both.
