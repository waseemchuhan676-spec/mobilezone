Prices

All prices are approximate market prices, marked with their last update date, and they change with the market, the dollar rate, and shop stock. The final price is whatever the shop charges at the time of purchase. MobileZone is not liable for a difference between a listed price and a shop price.

PTA tax estimates

PTA tax figures from the calculator are estimates based on the published FBR duty structure (FY 2026-27). The final payable duty depends on the FBR's official customs value of the device and on your registration route, and is confirmed only through the official PTA/DIRBS process. This site is not an official PTA tool.

Specifications

Only confirmed specifications are shown. Where a specification is not confirmed, the page says so instead of guessing. Always check the official brand specification sheet before buying, especially for display type, chipset, charging speed and warranty terms, which the catalogue does not yet list per phone.
