Privacy Policy

MobileZone is a static information site. It does not ask you to create an account, does not run tracking scripts, and does not set cookies of its own. This page states plainly what happens when you visit.

Data MobileZone collects

None. There is no sign-in, no form that submits personal data to MobileZone, and no analytics or advertising script on the site. The PTA tax calculator and phone finder run entirely in your browser: a phone price or IMEI you type into the calculator is never sent to any server and never stored.

Hosting

The site is served by a hosting provider (Vercel). Like any web host, its infrastructure may log basic request data (such as IP address and requested page) as part of delivering the site. MobileZone itself does not receive, use, or sell that data.

External links

Pages link to external sites (for example pta.gov.pk and brand websites). Those sites have their own privacy policies; MobileZone is not responsible for them. Opening an external link is governed by that site's policy, not this one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. The current version is dated 31 October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Does MobileZone track me?
No. There are no analytics or advertising scripts on the site, and MobileZone sets no cookies of its own.
Is my IMEI sent anywhere when I use the PTA calculator?
No. The calculator validates an IMEI only in your browser. It is never transmitted or stored.
Do you collect email addresses?
No. The site has no sign-in and no form that collects personal data.
