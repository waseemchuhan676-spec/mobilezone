PTA Tax Calculator

Estimate the PTA tax (DIRBS registration duty) on an imported mobile phone in Pakistan. Enter the phone's price, choose the registration route and filer status, and get an estimated breakdown. Rates: FBR FY 2026-27 structure, in force 1 July 2026 (regulatory duty per SRO 1064(I)/2026). Estimate only — DIRBS shows the exact figure for your IMEI.

Calculate your PTA tax

The duty bands (FBR FY 2026-27)

PTA tax is not a single percentage: it is built from regulatory duty (by value band), sales tax (18% up to $500, 25% above), a handset levy, and income tax on the CNIC route. On the passport route income tax is exempt; on the CNIC route non-ATL (non-filer) taxpayers pay double the income-tax component. Feature (non-smart) phones also carry a small customs duty of Rs. 250.

A worked example

A $600 smartphone (about PKR 168,000 at Rs. 280 per dollar) on the CNIC route as an ATL filer works out to roughly Rs. 79,100 in total duty; on the passport route the same phone is roughly Rs. 67,600, because income tax drops out. Try it in the calculator above — enter 600, choose USD, and compare the two routes. These are estimates from the published band structure — your final figure depends on the FBR's official customs value, which can differ from the shop price.

What the calculator cannot tell you

The calculator cannot confirm your eligibility, your device's official customs value, or whether a fine applies (a 10% fine may apply in some late-registration cases and is shown separately, never folded into the total). There is no duty-free allowance — every phone connecting to a Pakistani network must be registered and duty paid. The free facility is a 120-day temporary registration for overseas visitors (one phone, one SIM), after which the device is blocked. Final payable duties and taxes should be confirmed through the official applicable government/PTA process, and this tool is not an official PTA tool. Note: the DIRBS PSID is final and may include additional customs duty (about 4% is claimed by some sources, unconfirmed on any DIRBS PSID) — this calculator does not add it. Check your IMEI free: SMS it to 8484 or visit dirbs.pta.gov.pk.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate PTA tax on a mobile phone in Pakistan?
Enter the phone's price above (in PKR or USD), choose the registration route (Passport or CNIC) and filer status, then tap Calculate PTA Tax. The calculator applies the FBR FY 2026-27 band structure (regulatory duty, sales tax, handset levy and, on the CNIC route, income tax) and shows an estimated total. Confirm the official amount on the PTA DIRBS portal.
Is PTA tax a fixed percentage?
No. It is a sum of regulatory duty, sales tax, levy and (on the CNIC route) income tax, each depending on the phone's value band and your status.
What is the PTA tax on a $600 phone?
About Rs. 79,100 on the CNIC route as an ATL filer, or about Rs. 67,600 on the passport route (at Rs. 280 per dollar). These are estimates from the published band structure — your final figure depends on the FBR's official customs value, which can differ from the shop price.
Do I pay tax on a phone bought in Pakistan?
A phone already registered on DIRBS (PTA approved) needs no further duty. Tax applies when an unregistered imported phone is registered.
Can the shop's price be used as the customs value?
Not necessarily. Duty is charged on the FBR's official customs value, which may differ from what you paid.
