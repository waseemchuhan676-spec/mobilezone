How to Choose a Mobile Phone by Budget in Pakistan

Start from your budget, not from a flagship. Each price tier buys a different balance of performance, camera, battery and longevity — and the right phone is the one whose compromises you notice least.

Under Rs 50,000: the value tier

This is where most Pakistani buyers shop. The catalogue's under-Rs 50,000 list holds 12 phones, with the cheapest listed phone at Rs 25,899. Expect solid everyday performance, good battery, and a decent main camera — but check RAM (aim for 8GB if you can) and confirm 5G only if you need it, since it adds cost here.

Rs 50,000–100,000: the balanced tier

This tier buys smoother displays, better cameras with stabilisation, faster charging, and longer software support. It is the sweet spot if you keep a phone for three or more years. Use the under-100K list and compare 3 finalists side by side.

Above Rs 100,000: flagships and their cost

Flagships buy the best cameras, fastest chips and longest support — at a steep premium. Before stretching, ask what you actually gain over the balanced tier for your use (calls, social, photos, light gaming usually gain little).

Always add the total cost

Budget for the phone plus a case, and — for an imported or non-PTA phone — the PTA duty (estimate it with the PTA Tax Calculator). A phone that fits your budget before duty can break it after. Then shortlist with the Smart Finder and confirm the final price at a shop.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best budget for a phone in Pakistan?
There is no single answer. Under Rs 50,000 covers everyday use well; Rs 50,000–100,000 is the sweet spot for a phone kept several years.
Should I stretch my budget for a flagship?
Only if your use (heavy gaming, professional photos, long-term keeping) genuinely benefits. For typical use, the balanced tier is enough.
Does the listed price include PTA tax?
Listed prices are shop/market prices. If the phone is non-PTA, duty is extra — estimate it before deciding.
