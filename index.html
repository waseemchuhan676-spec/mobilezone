Mobile rates in Pakistan change with USD, import duty, PTA tax and local demand. Official warranty phones usually cost more than grey (unofficial) units. Always confirm the final price at your city shop and check PTA approval status before buying.

Mobile Prices Pakistan – FAQ

Common questions
What is the latest mobile price in Pakistan 2026?
Mobile prices change often. On MobileZone you can see updated rates for Samsung, iPhone, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, Infinix, Tecno and more. Use search or brand filters for the model you want, then confirm the final price at a local shop.
How to calculate PTA tax on a mobile phone in Pakistan?
Use the PTA Tax Calculator on this page: pick a phone or enter its price, choose Passport or CNIC registration and your filer status, and get an estimated breakdown (regulatory duty, sales tax, handset levy, income tax). For the official payable amount, always check your IMEI on the PTA DIRBS portal before paying.
What is the best phone under 30000 in Pakistan?
Under Rs. 30,000, look at Infinix, Tecno, Redmi, Sparx and entry Vivo/Samsung models. Use Smart Finder with budget “Under 30,000” and priority “Best Value”, or open the Under 20K / 20–30K price boxes above.
What is the best phone under 50000 in Pakistan?
Under Rs. 50,000 you get stronger mid-range options such as Redmi Note series, Infinix Note, Tecno Camon, Samsung A-series and Vivo Y series. Filter 30K–50K or use Smart Finder with “Under 50,000”.
Which 5G phones are available in Pakistan?
Most new mid-range and flagship phones support 5G. In Phone Finder select Network = 5G to list only 5G mobiles with current approximate prices in Pakistan.
Official vs grey mobile in Pakistan – what is the difference?
Official phones usually include local warranty and PTA approval. Grey (unofficial) phones are often cheaper but may require PTA tax and have limited warranty. Check PTA status and total cost before purchase.
